#### My AI agent team
I run G-SYNQ with a small team of AI agents. **They draft and check, and I approve.** Nothing goes out (no email, ad spend, post or application) without my yes.

- **Claude Code** builds and writes: client tools, site code, video renders, drafts
- **Codex** does independent code reviews, rechecks and rules research
- **Grok bots:** a Chief bot runs my morning board and the relay between agents. Specialist bots handle job scans, prospect research, Meta ad audits, creative briefs and an AI news radar.
- **Handoffs:** agents pass tasks through a shared Drive inbox with a fixed handoff format, and a check flags anything stuck
